## The problem (why people need this)

### Section title
WhatsApp is your shop. But it was never built to run a business.

### Section intro
Most growing businesses in Nigeria and across Africa do not lose customers because their product is bad. They lose them because WhatsApp becomes impossible to manage: too many chats, too little time, no system, and no memory of who wanted what.

### Pain point cards

**1. Messages pile up faster than you can answer**
- Customers expect instant replies. When you are in traffic, in a meeting, or asleep, unanswered chats turn into lost orders.
- Copy hook: *"Every unread message is money walking away."*

**2. You repeat yourself all day**
- Same price questions. Same delivery questions. Same "is it available?" over and over.
- You become the bottleneck for your own business.

**3. No single place to see your business**
- Chats live on your phone. Products live in your head or another app. Follow-ups live in your memory.
- There is no dashboard, no history, no system.

**4. You cannot scale without hiring**
- More orders should mean more revenue — not more stress, more missed messages, and more manual typing.

**5. Customers forget you if you forget them**
- Leads go cold. People who asked yesterday never get a follow-up today.
- Growth stalls because retention is manual.

## How it works

### Section title
Go live in four steps

### Steps

**Step 1 — Create your account**
Sign up free with email or Google. No payment required to start.

**Step 2 — Connect WhatsApp**
Link your WhatsApp by scanning a QR code from the dashboard. Your session stays active on the server.

**Step 3 — Set your business rules**
Add your business profile, default AI instructions, products, and per-contact rules. Save once — MessageBuddy remembers.

**Step 4 — Let MessageBuddy work**
Reply manually, draft with AI, or enable auto-reply and follow-ups. Watch analytics and credits as your business runs smoother.

### Reassurance line
You can start manually and turn on automation when you are ready. No forced setup maze.

## Objection handling (use in FAQ or near CTA)

**"Will AI sound fake?"**
You control instructions, tone, and notes per contact. AI drafts and auto-replies use your business context — and you can edit before sending.

**"Do I have to keep my laptop open?"**
Auto-reply and follow-ups run on the MessageBuddy server after WhatsApp is connected. Your browser does not need to stay open.

**"Is this only for big companies?"**
No. MessageBuddy is built for solo sellers and small teams who are already drowning in WhatsApp DMs.

**"What if I want to reply myself sometimes?"**
You can. Use the dashboard like a normal inbox, turn off AI per contact, or use AI only to draft.
